Primary user journeysIntent, screen sequence, and key interaction

Designed around decisions, not just data.

Three core tasks connect reporting to a clear recruiting action.

Journey 01 · Budget allocation

Choose the next channel to invest in

People lead checks performance, compares conversion with quality and cost, then prioritizes one channel to scale or test.

Overview→Channel table→Budget choice
Journey 02 · Candidate progression

Find a candidate and move them forward

Recruiter searches the pipeline, checks the candidate's source and stage, then records an interview, offer, or hire.

Candidates→Search / review→Next action
Journey 03 · Funnel diagnosis

Spot a bottleneck and ask why

Hiring manager reviews funnel counts, identifies a drop-off stage, then compares channel and candidate context before changing the process.

Overview funnel→Stage detail→Process change

UX PRINCIPLES

Orient quicklyConsistent workspace navigation, a clear date range, and a concise metric summary establish context.
Make comparisons fairShow volume, hire rate, quality, and cost together instead of rewarding volume alone.
Connect insight to actionEvery key screen suggests a useful next step and preserves the user's path back to the full overview.
